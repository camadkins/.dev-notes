Notes in this garden exist at one of three maturity levels.

## Polished notes

Complete, reviewed, and intended to be useful as standalone references. These have:
- A clear definition or introduction
- Structured sections with explanations
- Examples or diagrams where they help
- Accurate tags and frontmatter

Most published notes in `cs/dsa/` and `cs/pl/` aim for this level.

## Working notes

Published but incomplete. These contain real content - not just placeholders - but may be missing sections, lack examples, or need editing. They're usable but clearly in progress.

Working notes are published (`draft: false`) and visible on the site.

## Drafts

Unpublished outlines or stubs. These have `draft: true` in their frontmatter and are **not visible** on the published site. They exist in the repo as scaffolding for future writing.

Drafts become working notes when they have enough real content to be useful to a reader, and working notes become polished notes through review and revision.

[[off-watch/media/index|The Dolphin Lounge]] is the film and TV corner of the garden, named for the lounge a submariner earns access to once qualified. This note records how it is organized.

## What a note here is

One film or show per note: a short factual setup, my take, the lesson if there is one (written in the note, not the frontmatter), and [[cs/math/graph-theory|links back into the rest of the garden wherever the film touches something I already have a note on]]. Sourcing is light, the take is the point, and notes start as seeds and sharpen on a rewatch.

## Frontmatter

```yaml
watched: 2026-06-20      # when I first saw it
updated:                 # when I last revised the note (blank shows the watched date)
status: finished         # watching | finished (drives "Currently watching")
year: 1981               # the film's own facts
director: Wolfgang Petersen
rating: 8                # optional, out of 10
maturity: seed           # seed | working | published
tags: [media, film, submarine]
```

## How the index is organized

- **Currently watching** lists what has `status: watching`.
- **Recommended** is a short hand-picked list of the ones worth someone's time.
- The **full list** at the bottom of the index is generated by Quartz (alphabetical) and includes everything regardless of status.

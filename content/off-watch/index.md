On the boat, off watch is the time that is yours. You have stood your hours, the systems are someone else's problem for a while, and you get to do the things that keep you human: watch a movie, read, think about something other than the mission. This is the part of the garden for that.

Three rooms off this passageway. Each is its own logbook, held to the same standard as the rest of the garden.

### Rooms

- [[off-watch/media/index|The Dolphin Lounge]] - films and shows, watched and judged by someone who earned the dolphins.
- [[off-watch/golf/index|The Clubhouse]] - the science of golf, course reviews, and a running bucket list.
- [[off-watch/books/index|The Ship's Library]] - books read and worth reading, with the take and the lesson.
- [[off-watch/dnd/index|Dungeons & Dragons]] - campaigns, sessions, characters, and lore, kept as a connected wiki.
- [[off-watch/games/index|The Mess Table]] - cards and board games, with house rules, strategy, and a running score log.

### Why it is here

The off-watch stuff is not separate from the work. The films teach something, the books point back into [[cs/index|the CS notes]], and the discipline that earned the dolphins is the same discipline behind all of it. This is where those threads start.

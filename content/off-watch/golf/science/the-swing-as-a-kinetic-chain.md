## The question

Where does clubhead speed actually come from? Not the arms. The swing is a kinetic chain: large segments fire first and pass energy up to smaller, faster ones, so the clubhead arrives fastest at the bottom. I want the sourced version of the sequence and why it matters more than strength.

## What to research

- The ground-up sequence: ground reaction force, hips, torso, lead arm, then hands and club.
- Proximal-to-distal sequencing and the "kinematic sequence" that launch monitors graph.
- Why early release (casting) leaks speed, and how lag preserves it.
- The role of the X-factor (hip-shoulder separation) and the evidence for and against it.

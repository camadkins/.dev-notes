## The question

Why does a dimpled golf ball fly roughly twice as far as a smooth ball hit the same way? The short version everyone repeats is "the dimples reduce drag," which is true but incomplete: the dimples also let backspin generate lift. I want the full, sourced version.

## What to research

- The boundary layer: how dimples trip the airflow into turbulence and shrink the low-pressure wake behind the ball (pressure drag).
- Backspin and lift: how spin plus the dimpled surface produces an upward aerodynamic force that keeps the ball airborne.
- The trade between drag reduction and lift, and where dimple count and depth actually land in modern balls.
- A clean force diagram: gravity, drag, lift, and the resulting carry curve.
